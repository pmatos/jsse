jsse HEAD vs boa 0.22 — performance

jsse is still slower than boa 0.22 on most workloads: 1.65× geomean on the micro-benchmarks (7 of 9 slower, 1 tie, 1 faster) and a 2.2× lower JetStream 3 score geomean across the 44 benchmarks both engines run. jsse completes all 48 JetStream workloads; boa errors on 4. jsse wins on code-load, JSON and regexp-heavy workloads and loses worst on call-, closure-, promise- and collection-heavy ones.

Method and caveats

JetStream was run with --iterations 1, so scores are dominated by cold, first-iteration cost (parse, startup, first execution) rather than steady-state throughput. That favors jsse on the code-load benchmarks and may understate boa's lead elsewhere. Boa's four JetStream errors (Basic, typescript-octane, OfflineAssembler, validatorjs) all print only execution failed; the cause was not investigated. The micro-benchmark gap is smaller than the JetStream gap, so the headline number depends on the workload mix. The bytecode VM (default in jsse HEAD) matters most on opmix: 31 s versus 79 s for --no-bytecode.
  • Host: buildbox3 (AMD EPYC 7501, 2.0 GHz, load average ≈ 1–2 during runs); jsse built with nightly rustc, boa with stable, both cargo build --release with default profiles.
  • Micro-benchmark spread (max ÷ min of 5 runs) was ≤ 1.06 for every script except regex (≈ 1.4 on both engines, sub-0.2 s runs).
  • JetStream: jsse and boa ran concurrently on separate cores of the same host.
  • Reproduce: python3 microbench.py <engines-json> benchmarks/scripts out.json 5 and scripts/run-jetstream.py --engine <bin> --iterations 1 --timeout 120 --no-idle-gate.
